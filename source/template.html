Scope & limitations

Automatic matching uses text, visual fingerprints and document order. Blank or repetitive sheets and heavily revised pages can be ambiguous; review flagged matches. Reordered sheets may appear as removals/additions. No OCR, part-number matching, vector editing or collaboration. PDF annotations and comments are excluded. Visual regions are review aids, not verified engineering changes. Maximum 250,000 page-pair candidates; 12 megapixels per rendered sheet. Only local, unencrypted PDFs are supported.

Uses Mozilla PDF.js 6.4.299. Custom application code is MIT licensed; bundled components retain their own licenses in the evaluation package.
