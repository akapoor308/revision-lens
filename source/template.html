Scope & limits

Matching uses extracted text, a visual fingerprint and page order. Blank, repeated or heavily redrawn sheets can be ambiguous — check anything flagged. Moved sheets usually read as one removal plus one addition.

No OCR, CAD entity recognition or calibrated measurement. Annotations and form fields are outside the comparison. Limits: 150 MB per PDF, 250,000 candidate page pairs, 12 megapixels per sheet, 1,500 text lines per sheet.
